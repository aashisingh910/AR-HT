## What the app does with your camera
HomeTown AR uses your camera to show furniture in your room. The camera picture is processed **on your phone** and is **not sent to us**. A photo or video is only stored
when you take one, and it is saved to your phone's Photos. The app does not use your microphone (videos are silent), your location or your contacts.

## What we collect and why
- **Account (optional).** If you create an account we store your email, your name if you give it, and a protected form of your password (we cannot read it). If you sign in with Google or Apple we store that account's id and the email Apple/Google shares. We use this to run your account and back up your designs.
- **Designs and saved furniture.** If you are signed in and have verified your email, the pieces, positions, names, room type and the size of a room you scanned (width, depth, area) are backed up to our server so you can use them on other phones. Without an account they stay on your phone.
- **Design photos.** A photo of a design is a screenshot of the AR view, so it shows your room. We upload it **only** when you share that design, or when you turn on "Back up design photos" in Settings (off by default). A shared design's page is public to anyone who has its link; you can stop sharing at any time.
- **Anonymous usage.** Unless you turn off "Share anonymous usage" in Settings, the app sends which features are used (for example "AR opened", "piece placed") with product ids and numbers, and a random install id that is not linked to your account. No images, no room shapes.
- **Crash reports.** If the app crashes it can send a report with the error, app version and phone model. Emails, tokens and passwords are removed before it leaves your phone.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and sites.

## Who sees your data
Our hosting provider ([host]) stores the server data, and our crash reporting provider (Sentry) receives crash reports. They act on our instructions. Shared design links are public by design.

## How long we keep it
Account data and designs are kept until you delete them or your account. Anonymous usage data is kept for [12] months. Crash reports are kept for [90] days.

## Your choices and rights
- Delete everything: Settings, Delete my data (signed in or not), or visit https://[your domain]/account/delete. This removes your account, designs, saved furniture, linked sign-ins and stored photos.
- Stop sharing a design: delete the design or ask us to revoke the link.
- Turn off anonymous usage and photo backup in Settings.
- Access, correct or export your data, or object to processing: contact [privacy email]. [Add local-law rights, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.]
